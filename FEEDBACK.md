## Why Proof of Human is proportionate

Borrow From A Human does not need a passport, nationality, legal name, or age. The product event is a stranger making a physical-world borrowing promise. Proof of Human is the minimum sufficient credential because the application only needs to know that a real human stands behind each side of the agreement.
